Fibonacci retracement levels are one of the few technical tools that show up consistently across every liquid market — forex, equities, futures, crypto — because enough participants watch the same levels that they become somewhat self-fulfilling.

## Why 61.8% Matters

The golden ratio level (61.8%) is the most closely watched retracement because it represents the boundary between a healthy pullback and a genuine trend reversal. Price reacting cleanly at this level, especially with a rejection candle and rising volume, is one of the higher-probability continuation signals in technical analysis.

## Combining Fibonacci with Structure

Fibonacci levels alone are not a strategy — they are a *zone of interest*. The edge comes from combining a retracement level with an independent piece of confluence: a prior support/resistance level, a round number, or a volume node. When two or three of these line up at the same price, that zone deserves your attention; a lone Fibonacci level with nothing else backing it up does not.

## A Simple Entry Framework

1. Mark the most recent significant swing high and swing low.
2. Watch for price to retrace into the 50%–61.8% zone.
3. Wait for a rejection signal (engulfing candle, pin bar, or a clear loss of downward momentum) before entering — never enter simply because price *touched* the level.
4. Place your stop beyond the 78.6% level, since a break past that point usually invalidates the retracement thesis entirely.

Used this way, Fibonacci retracements stop being a lagging curiosity and become a genuinely useful entry-timing tool.
